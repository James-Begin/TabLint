## Limitations

- The gradient-search advantage (H1/H4) was supported on 3 of 7 pre-registered dataset tests (breast cancer, eeg-eye-state, MagicTelescope) and not on German credit, Taiwan (12 and 40 steps), phoneme or mozilla4.
- TabPFN leave-one-out did not beat logistic-regression leave-one-out as a detector in any pre-registered regime (H5).
- Constraints are geometric/schema checks, not domain realism. Appended labels are adversarially corrupted.
- A failed search is not a robustness certificate; k = 3 is a fixed budget, not a minimum.
- Transfer analyses use rows optimised for TabPFN; they do not measure the robustness of other models under attacks optimised against them.
- Public research datasets; no clinical or lending conclusions.
