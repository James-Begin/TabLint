# Proofreading famous datasets

Exploratory scan with a protocol fixed in advance (`docs/FAMOUS_DATASETS_PREREG.md`). Proofread (TabPFN-3.5, default threshold 2) was run on 8 well-known tables. For each table, **all top-10 flags are listed in rank order**. A flag is called a *verified error* only with a cited source or a physical or definitional argument; everything else is *plausible but unusual* (shown to be real) or *unverified*. Generated by `benchmarks/make_famous_gallery.py`.

Totals over 8 datasets: **8 verified errors**, 2 plausible but unusual, 61 unverified (not checked or not checkable). The UCI Auto MPG Buick (demo set) is a further verified error.

**Known-answer check (pre-specified, UCI Iris).** The two errors documented in UCI's `iris.names` were **not flagged**:
- sample 35 petal_width = 0.1: surprise 0.52 (threshold 2), not listed
- sample 38 sepal_width = 3.1: surprise 0.17 (threshold 2), not listed
- sample 38 petal_length = 1.5: surprise 0.17 (threshold 2), not listed

These are 0.1–0.5 cm slips that sit inside natural flower-to-flower variation, so nothing in the row contradicts them. Proofread catches values that are implausible given the rest of the row, not small plausible ones.

- Rows are 0-based positions in the source table. For tables with more than 10,000 rows, a fixed 10,000-row sample (seed 0) plus every row with an impossible zero was scanned, as pre-registered.
- "Unverified" does not mean wrong or right. These are prompts a data owner would check.
- Data are loaded by URL from their public sources and are not redistributed here.
