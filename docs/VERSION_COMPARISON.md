# TabPFN version comparison

TabLint cell-error benchmark, fresh seeds 801–805, 12 datasets, 60 tables; same injection and scoring as H6. Generated by `benchmarks/summarize_versions.py`. Two dataset executions were removed after reported test errors; see [the amendment](BENCHMARK_AMENDMENT.md).

| | TabPFN v2 | TabPFN v3 | TabPFN-3.5 | TabPFN-3.5-Fast |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Mean precision@k (12 datasets) | 0.710 | 0.736 | 0.738 | 0.734 |
| Total GPU seconds (all tables) | 2592 | 1605 | 2565 | 1035 |

- **H9, TabPFN-3.5 > v2:** wins 11, losses 1 of 12; mean +0.029 [+0.015, +0.046]; one-sided Wilcoxon p = 0.00049. supported (alpha 0.025).
- **H10, TabPFN-3.5 > v3:** wins 5, losses 7 of 12; mean +0.002 [-0.008, +0.013]; p = 0.48. not supported.
- **TabPFN-3.5-Fast vs 3.5** (descriptive): mean precision@k -0.005; lower on 9/12 datasets; median speed-up 2.7×.
